How to adjust

  • Keep calories at 2,700 if average weight is trending down and you feel okay.
  • If there is no meaningful loss for 2–3 weeks and food tracking is consistent, consider reducing by around 150–200 kcal/day.
  • If weight drops quickly and you feel depleted or weaker, consider adding 150–250 kcal/day and reviewing recovery.
  • If weight is stable but waist is shrinking and strength is improving, usually keep the plan unchanged.
